Evaluation Protocol & Metric Definitions

SR: Stable Success Rate (%). LSCR: Latched Stage Completion Rate (%). Eff.: Success-Conditioned Efficiency. SR and LSCR use all 26 tasks equally and retain the paper’s reported precision. Coverage counts complete task records for the selected ranking metric. A documented four-channel SR or LSCR mean remains comparable when its channel breakdown is pending. Missing channel values are never inferred from the mean. Partial evaluations are unranked (—).

Efficiency is the mean steps to successful task completion across human expert teleoperation trajectories for the task, divided by mean steps to stable success pooled across successful policy episodes in all four channels. All four visual–proprioceptive baselines use executed physics-step counts and recorded task-level expert references. It is not an average of channel ratios. Paper baseline efficiency summaries include ACT: 25 tasks, DP: 19, π₀.₅: 23, and GR00T N1.5: 25. These successful-task counts appear beneath Eff.; coverage also includes explicitly reported no-success task records.

NR denotes results not reported. — means no successful rollout; Not evaluated and N/A indicate distinct missing-data states. Zero is a reported value. Comparisons use the same task set, episode budget, termination rules, and expert reference within each evaluation group. Input variants are labeled separately.

The 20,800 rollouts refer to the four published visual–proprioceptive baselines. The GR00T N1.5 tactile variant adds 5,200 rollouts and reports four-channel mean SR only; channel SR, LSCR, and efficiency are not reported. The 50 rollouts per task–policy–channel are evaluation episodes, not independent retraining replicates. Tasks and embodiments are not factorially crossed, so task-level contrasts do not isolate embodiment effects.
