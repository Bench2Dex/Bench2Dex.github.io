Evaluation Protocol & Metric Definitions

SR: episodes reaching stable success (%). LSCR: dependency-valid task stages ever reached (%). Overall SR and LSCR weight all 26 tasks and four channels equally.

Eff.: mean expert completion steps divided by mean policy steps to stable success among successful episodes, pooled across channels. Overall Eff. averages tasks with success (ACT: 25; DP: 19; π₀.₅: 23; each GR00T variant: 25).

Protocol. Bench2Dex evaluations use 50 episodes per task and channel. GPT-6-Astra uses ICL and three episodes per task on Full only; 1× / 10× indicate execution-step budgets. Its Full SR is not a four-channel mean. External evaluation settings differ. Sources: Phoenix · GPT-6-Astra.

NR: not reported. — in Rank: no score for the selected metric; in Eff.: no successful rollout.
